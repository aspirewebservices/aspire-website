Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to Aspire Web Services ("we," "our," or "us"). We are dedicated to maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and security of your digital personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we gather, process, retain, and safeguard personal information collected through our official website (aspirewebservices.in) and related software services, including Aspire ERP, in alignment with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and the Information Technology Act.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Voluntarily

  • Direct Inquiries: When submitting forms or chatting with us on WhatsApp, you provide your full name, email address, phone number, and project or demo specifications.
  • Client Relationship & Billing Data: Upon onboarding for website development, SEO, branding, or hosting assistance, we collect corporate billing details and server or registrar credentials required to deliver work.
  • Aspire ERP Institutional Records: When schools deploy Aspire ERP, designated administrators record student, teacher, and parent profiles. We process and secure this data strictly as a technical intermediary and Data Processor under the school's explicit direction.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Server Log & Network Telemetry: Web servers automatically log visitor IP addresses, browser specifications, OS types, referring URLs, and access timestamps.
  • Essential & Analytics Cookies: We utilize essential session tokens and analytics cookies to optimize page response times and evaluate user engagement patterns.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process personal data solely for legitimate, contracted purposes:

  • To develop, design, host, and maintain custom web products.
  • To respond to service quotes, project inquiries, and provide ongoing technical assistance.
  • To generate commercial estimates, invoices, and maintain accounting records.
  • To prevent cybersecurity incidents, fraud, unauthorized portal access, and server exploits.
  • To comply with regulatory standards and lawful requests under Indian statutory law.

3. Third-Party Sharing & Processing

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal details. Data is disclosed only under these operational requirements:

  • Technical Service Providers: Verified cloud server providers, SMS/WhatsApp notification APIs, form handlers (Formspree), and hosting datacenters working under confidentiality obligations.
  • Statutory Compliance: When compelled by court orders or governmental enforcement agencies under applicable Indian jurisdiction.
  • Asset Transfers: In the event of an organizational restructuring or merger, data will remain bound to equivalent data protection safeguards.

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard protective protocols, including 256-bit TLS/SSL encryption in transit, strict database access controls, network firewalls, and credential protection. Access to institutional databases (such as student or parent accounts in Aspire ERP) is partitioned through Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).

5. Data Retention & Erasure

We retain personal information only for the duration necessary to satisfy contracted objectives, resolve customer support inquiries, or fulfill statutory accounting obligations. Once the data lifecycle expires, records are permanently deleted or irreversibly anonymized.

6. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act

As a Data Principal, you possess statutory rights concerning the personal data under our custody:

  • Right to Access: Request a summary of the personal information being processed.
  • Right to Correction: Request corrections, amendments, or completions of inaccurate records.
  • Right to Erasure: Request the deletion of personal data that is no longer required for legal or contractual fulfillment.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: Direct questions, concerns, or complaints regarding data processing directly to our Grievance Officer.

7. External Links

Our website may reference external destinations, such as client portfolio URLs, partner platforms, and third-party social profiles. We do not govern and are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or tracking mechanisms employed by external domains.
